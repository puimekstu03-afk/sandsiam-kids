# SANDSIAM

## Register
product

## Users
Thai, English-speaking and Simplified Chinese-speaking visitors creating a personal Songkran sand pagoda on phones and computers. The original task requests a complete five-step creative game.

## Product Purpose
Choose a courtyard, build a sand pagoda, arrange decorations, learn about the tradition and write a blessing, then export an image or share the complete design through a link. Preserve work when switching language and between visits.

## Brand Personality
Warm, handcrafted, welcoming. Continue the user's selected miniature 2.5D concept and existing cream, sand, teal and coral interface. The approved reference is the original task's SANDSIAM desktop/mobile concept image.

## Anti-references
Avoid unrelated dashboard styling, aggressive animation and paywalls. Keep the artwork central and controls familiar.

## Design Principles
1. Make the pagoda the focus.
2. Offer tap and keyboard alternatives to dragging.
3. Preserve work and allow undo.
4. Translate the full workflow.
5. Make sharing and local storage limitations clear.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable text, visible focus, labeled controls, reduced motion, responsive layout, native form controls, keyboard decoration placement and manipulation.
